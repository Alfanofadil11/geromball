Privacy Policy

Geromball Clip Publisher · geromball.com · Last updated: 23 November 2026

Geromball Clip Publisher ("the App") is an internal tool operated by PT Satulimasatu Media Bersama ("Geromball", "we") to publish short video clips from Geromball's own football talk show, Pojok Debat, to TikTok accounts that are managed by our team. The App is not offered to the general public.

Information we access from TikTok

When a TikTok account holder connects their account and authorizes the App, we access only what the requested permissions allow:

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar, to show which account a post will be published to.
  • Creator posting information: the privacy options, interaction settings and maximum video length TikTok allows for the account, so the account holder can choose them before each post.
  • Video upload and publish (video.upload, video.publish): to upload a video clip and publish it with the caption and settings the account holder approved.
  • Public video list and statistics (video.list, where granted): to show the account holder how published clips perform.

We do not access direct messages, contacts, followers' personal data, or any content the account holder did not publish through the App.

How we use it

  • To publish the clips the account holder reviewed and approved, at the time they scheduled.
  • To show the publish status and basic performance (views, likes) of those clips inside the App.

We do not sell, rent or share TikTok data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted by our credential provider and are never shown to App users. Post records (which clip was published to which account, when, and its public link and view counts) are stored on our own server. The App is reachable only by Geromball team members with a personal login.

Retention and deletion

Post records are kept while the account remains connected and for up to 12 months after. An account holder can disconnect the App at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services) or by asking us; we then delete the stored tokens within 7 days and, on request, the post records.
