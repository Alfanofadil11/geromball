1. The service

Geromball Clip Publisher ("the App") is an internal publishing tool of PT Satulimasatu Media Bersama ("Geromball"). It prepares short clips from Geromball's own show, Pojok Debat, and publishes them to TikTok accounts managed by the Geromball team, after the account holder reviews and approves each post. Access is limited to Geromball team members.

2. Account holder responsibilities

  • Connect only TikTok accounts you are authorized to manage.
  • Review each clip, caption, privacy setting and interaction setting before approving it; nothing is published without that approval.
  • Follow TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, Music Usage Confirmation and, where relevant, Branded Content Policy.

3. Content

Clips come from programs Geromball produces. Geromball is responsible for having the rights needed to publish the content the App prepares. The account holder may edit the caption or decline any clip.

4. Disconnecting

The account holder may disconnect the App at any time in TikTok or by contacting us. Scheduled posts that were not yet published are cancelled when an account is disconnected.
